    2 · Output

    How this works. An SX/A card holds plain WAVs, so they are read straight off it and the pad each one sits on comes from PAD_INFO.BIN. The MKII keeps its samples in internal memory, so export them to the card from the unit first (EXPORT SAMPLE) and this tool tidies up what it wrote. Left on “keep original” with nothing ticked, the audio is copied through sample for sample — only the Roland header is dropped and the file renamed.
