Plugins built here. Synthesisers, effects racks, a Euclidean drum machine, an analyser, a sample tuner.

Every one of these has a simulation you can play right here, in the page. It is not the plugin — a browser cannot load a VST3, and anything claiming otherwise is running something else. Each simulation is a small WebAudio instrument built from that plugin’s own controls, close enough to tell you whether you want the real one. Every one lists what it leaves out.

Each thing here is sold by whoever made it, and pays them directly. Checkout is Stripe’s own hosted page — no card detail ever reaches this site — and the artist’s share goes to their account at the moment you pay rather than being passed on later. You get a download link that works for a few days and a handful of goes; if you lose it, write and it will be reissued.
