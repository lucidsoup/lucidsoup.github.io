Visual Playground

Six WebGL scenes driven by whatever you are listening to, with the knobs out where you can reach them. Pick where the sound comes from — everything after that happens on this machine, and nothing is uploaded or recorded.

01 A link A direct audio link or a radio stream plays straight through. A page — SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Mixcloud and a thousand others — goes via the same service the SP-404 converter uses. Two it cannot help with: a station that streams through its own player, and YouTube, which refuses servers it does not recognise. For those, share the tab. Works in every browser.

The shared service gives every address twenty clips an hour, because it is one machine paid for by one person. Run your own and the limit is whatever you set it to — it is one container in ripper/ with no state and no database. This is the same setting the SP-404 converter uses, so filling it in here fills it in there.
