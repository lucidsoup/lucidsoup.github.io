BlackBox probe

Reads what an M-VAVE ANN BlackBox tells your computer about itself, over USB-C or Bluetooth: its USB identity, its interfaces (audio, MIDI, the update channel), its Bluetooth services, its firmware identity and the MIDI it sends when you use it. For SynthSoup's research (synthsoup/research/blackbox).

Read-only. This page never writes to the pedal. It reads the USB descriptors your browser already has, and over MIDI it sends one question only, the identity query M-VAVE's own updater asks (F0 00 32 45 00 00 00 40 7F F7), plus the standard MIDI identity request (F0 7E 7F 06 01 F7). Over Bluetooth it only reads and listens: it never writes to the pedal's data channel (AE41), and over BLE-MIDI it sends the same identity query only if you press Ask. It never sends the update command or anything else. The serial number is left out of the report.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Safari doesn't have WebUSB, WebHID, Web MIDI or Web Bluetooth). Plug the BlackBox in with a data USB-C cable and switch it on. Close M-EFCS and other music apps first. Bluetooth only (step 4) also works in Chrome on Android, or on an iPhone in the Bluefy browser.

1. USB identity and interfaces

Pick the BlackBox in the list your browser shows. If it isn't listed there, skip this step; steps 2 and 3 still work.

2. The update channel (HID)

The pedal's update and editor channel shows up as a HID device. Pick the BlackBox; the page reads only its report layout.

3. MIDI: identity and what it sends

Allow MIDI (with SysEx) when the browser asks. Then Ask identity. After that, press each button, footswitch and knob on the pedal one at a time: everything it sends appears below.

The question goes only to the output picked here (the BlackBox's, when its name is recognised), never to other gear.

4. Bluetooth

Disconnect the pedal from the M-EFCS app on your phone first (it allows one connection). Then pick the BlackBox in the Bluetooth list. The page lists its services, reads what is readable and listens to its data channel (AE42) and its BLE-MIDI. Use the pedal's controls and watch.
