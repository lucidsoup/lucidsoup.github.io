Vocoder

Hold to glide: hold a key, then press another, and the pitch glides to it (on a phone, hold with one finger and tap with another; on a computer keyboard, hold one letter and press the next). Let go of the new key and it glides back to the one still held. It sets the track to LEG with a smooth 0.4 s glide (EXP: quick at first, easing into the note); press it again for the track's own settings back. Sliding a finger or the mouse along the keys glides too, and the Glide slider sets how slow (at the top: 1.5 s).

Turns this track's INSERT into the VOCODER with your microphone as the voice: play chords and talk or sing. Use headphones, or the speakers feed back into the mic. On the FM-1 itself the voice is another track (INSERT › VOCOD, SRC TRK1–4), for example a recording loaded into a sample slot.

MIDI
Switch on to connect MIDI

Unticked: channels 1–4 play tracks 1–4. Program Change picks presets, CC74 / CC71 are cutoff and resonance, CC7 level, CC91 / CC94 reverb and delay sends.

Granular
 

Turns the selected track into the GRAIN engine on any sample, your own USR1–3 included. Each key plays a cloud of tiny grains: POS is where in the sample they come from, SIZE how long each grain is, DENSITY how many, SPRAY how far they scatter. Hold a note and move POS to scrub through the sound.

Samples

Load your own sounds from an SD card (in a card reader, or the Files app on a phone), your computer or cloud storage: WAV, MP3, M4A or AIFF. Each slot holds up to 7.4 s; silence at the start is trimmed, stereo is mixed to mono. A loaded sample plays at once on the selected track (the SAMPLE engine, middle C at its own pitch); GRAIN and SLICE can use the slots too. Drop files on a slot, or several on the panel to fill the slots in order.

What's in the soup

Switch on, wait for the cat, then try these. The Sound panel under the synth picks any engine and preset for each track, and POLY plays chords. Keys in brackets are the computer-keyboard shortcuts. Tap FX one press at a time; a quick double tap does something else.

  • TABLE wavetables

    hold EDIT 5 + tap A3 C

    A morphing wavetable engine with 16 tables and 10 warp modes. KNOB 1 (POS) scans through the table; PRESETS has 16 sounds to start from.

  • SID chip

    hold EDIT 5 + tap B3 V

    The Commodore 64's sound chip, from Melodee: three oscillators per note, the real chip's filter, and 32 presets.

  • DIMENSION: grains and tape

    tap FX 1 six times, then KNOB 1

    The delay's mode: DELAY, DIMEN (a granular cloud with octave shimmer and freeze) or TAPE (wow, flutter, drive and a worn, hissing tape). The track's delay send is on the first FX page, KNOB 3.

  • SHIMMER reverb

    tap FX 1 seven times, then KNOB 1

    A fourth reverb type: the hall's tail climbs in octaves. Turn up the track's reverb send (first FX page, KNOB 4) and hold a chord.

  • RESAMPLE

    hold REC = + tap C4 B / D4 N / E4 M

    Records what the synth plays into user sample slot 1, 2 or 3. Play, tap the same key again to stop, then stop the transport and it saves. Play it back with the SAMPLE, SLICE or GRAIN engine.

  • The soup cat

    hold HOME 7 for the menu

    The power-on animation and the SOUP colours. MENU > DISPLAY has ANIM to turn the cat off and COLOR for Felucca's other palettes.
