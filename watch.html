Watchlist

Films, series and shorts worth an artist's time — what to take from each one rather than whether it is good. Three lanes: how a thing was made to look that way, the psychedelic lineage this place comes out of, and people making things. Search it, filter it, argue with it.

About this list

It lives in watch.json and the page reads nothing else, so adding to it is editing one file. Every entry is a title, year and director stated with confidence; where a cinematographer or a credit was uncertain the field was left out rather than guessed at. There are no posters and no third-party requests — nothing here phones anywhere.
