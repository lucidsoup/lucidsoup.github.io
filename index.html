SP-404 Converter

Drop any audio in and get a card-ready ZIP out — right sample rate, right filenames, and a merged PAD_INFO.BIN for the SP-404SX and SP-404A, or an IMPORT folder for the SP-404MKII. It goes the other way too: point it at a card and get your samples back as clean, pad-named WAVs, trimmed to the points the pads actually play. It all runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
