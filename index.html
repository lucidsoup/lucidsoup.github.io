DrumSeek — Live Beat Sequencer

9-track drum machine with synthesizers, effects, and MIDI control. The visuals in the background are reactively driven by DrumSeek's audio output.

SP-404 Converter

Drop any audio in and get a card-ready ZIP out — right sample rate, right filenames, and a merged PAD_INFO.BIN for the SP-404SX and SP-404A, or an IMPORT folder for the SP-404MKII. It goes the other way too: point it at a card and get your samples back as clean, pad-named WAVs, trimmed to the points the pads actually play. It all runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Plugin Host

Your own CLAP and VST3 plugins, loaded on your machine and played in your browser. Pick a plugin, drop a sound on it, drag out a region and move the knobs — it re-renders as you go and hands you a WAV at the end, ready to chop. A browser cannot open a plugin itself, so a small program does that part and serves the page that drives it; it runs on your computer and talks to nothing else.

How to run it

SynthSoup

Custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: morphing wavetables, the Commodore 64's SID chip, granular and tape delays, a shimmer reverb that climbs in octaves, and sampling on the device. The whole instrument also runs in your browser — the same code, compiled to WebAssembly — so you can play it without an FM-1.

BlackboxFX Control

Turns the M-VAVE ANN BlackBox into a moving, morphing effects box, straight from your browser over USB or Bluetooth. Animate any knob with tempo-synced LFOs and drifting random, sweep between two sounds with one slider, recall scenes and reorder the chain. It only makes live changes, so reloading the preset on the pedal undoes everything. Needs Chrome or Edge; there's a simulator if you don't have the pedal with you.

About

I'm LucidSoup — I put light on things that weren't built to carry it. Buildings, stages, gallery walls. Most of the work is real time: shaders and patches taking live audio and movement as their input, so the piece answers the room it is standing in rather than playing back something decided weeks ago.

The other half is tools. Every time a job needed something that didn't exist I wrote it, and then finished it far enough to hand to somebody else. That's most of what's floating in the bowl at the top of this page, and all of it runs on your own machine.

Live & architectural

Facades, stages and rooms. Content cut for the surface it is going to land on and mapped on site, running against the set instead of beside it.
