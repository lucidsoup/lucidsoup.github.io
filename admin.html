The storefront is live again, at /store.html. What you edit here is what a visitor sees: that page reads this same items.json, and the bowl, the menu and the Stack all route to it. Fields this editor does not show — kind, format, platforms, demo — are carried through untouched, so editing a plugin here will not strip the line that tells the store which simulation to open.

Two things to know about ids. Any id that is all digits gets renumbered on export, so the plugins use word ids (mindbloom, hydra) and are safe. And prices are deliberately empty: every plugin card says “price not set” until you put one here.

Getting paid. Checkout is real now: Stripe’s own hosted page, with each artist’s share going to their Stripe account at the moment of the sale rather than passing through anyone. Each item names an artist; each artist sets up their own payouts through Stripe’s onboarding under Artists & payouts below. Their bank details never touch this page or the repository.

This needs the checkout worker deployed — see checkout/README.md. Until checkout.json has a base, the store falls back to plain links and shows no BUY buttons, which is a working shop and not a broken one. The price still comes from the price field, but it is now read by the worker rather than sent by the browser, because a price the browser sends is a price the buyer picks.

How it works: Edit any field below — changes save automatically to your browser as you type. When you're done, click SAVE / EXPORT JSON to download an updated items.json, then upload that file to the root of your repository.

Artists & payouts

Each artist sets up their own Stripe account through Stripe’s own onboarding — their bank details never come near this page or this repository. Once Stripe says an account can take charges, their items get a real BUY button and their share of every sale lands in their account at the moment it is paid.
