A browser cannot open a .clap, a .vst3 or a .component — they are native libraries, and no browser will load one. pluginhost does that part and serves this page, which is why the address is 127.0.0.1 and not your site: a page on an https:// site cannot reach a local helper on Safari at all, and Chrome 142 began asking permission first.
